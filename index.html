Grid Puzzle Walkthroughs

Step-by-step video solutions to the logic grid puzzles in our puzzle books. Each video takes one puzzle and solves it one step at a time, with no guessing, so a reader who got stuck can see exactly how every square is filled in.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@GridPuzzleWalkthroughs

Puzzle Walkthroughs Uploader

Puzzle Walkthroughs Uploader is the channel's own software. It is used only by the channel's owner, on the owner's computer, to upload the channel's own walkthrough videos to YouTube. It is not offered to anyone else.

It uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload the channel's walkthrough videos and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and privacy setting;
  • keep one playlist for each puzzle, holding that puzzle's current video, so a link printed in a book always opens the right video;
  • read the channel's name, and the details of its own videos and playlists, to check each upload went to the right place.

It reads nothing about viewers or any other YouTube user. See the privacy policy for what it stores and how to revoke its access.
